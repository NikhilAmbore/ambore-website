FAQ

Common questions

Ambore is a free, all-in-one AI career platform with 14 tools: a Job Portal (50,000+ hourly-updated jobs from 97 companies), AI Resume Builder, Cover Letter Generator, Email Generator, LinkedIn Optimizer, Portfolio Builder, and an AI Interview Co-Pilot for real-time answers during interviews.
Jobs are automatically scraped from 10 sources: LinkedIn, Indeed, USAJobs.gov, Adzuna, The Muse, Remotive, and more. New listings are refreshed every hour across 97 top companies.
Upload your existing resume and paste the job description. The AI analyzes both, rewrites your resume to highlight relevant skills, match keywords, and optimize formatting for ATS systems. You get a tailored resume in seconds.
Ambore runs entirely in your browser. There are no extensions, no screen sharing, and no external indicators. It works with Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, and in-person interviews.
Everything is free — no credit card, no trial limits. All personal data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to external servers beyond the AI API calls needed to generate answers.
We use Google Analytics with IP anonymization. Your IP is never stored or logged by us. We do not sell, share, or store IP addresses.
Nothing happens to you. Ambore stores zero user data on servers — your account and all content live entirely in your own browser's localStorage. There is no central database to steal.
Your password is hashed with SHA-256 before saving — the actual password is never stored anywhere, only a fingerprint of it. Even with localStorage access, your password cannot be recovered.
