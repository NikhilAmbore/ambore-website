Ambore is a free, all-in-one AI career platform with 14 tools: a Job Portal (50,000+ hourly-updated jobs from 97 companies), AI Resume Tailor, Cover Letter Generator, Email Generator, LinkedIn Optimizer, Portfolio Builder, and an AI Mock Interview tool for practicing answers.
Yes, completely free. No credit card, no trial limits, no paywalls on core features. A Pro plan is coming for power users who want priority AI and advanced features, but the free plan gives you everything you need to land a job.
Upload your existing resume and paste the job description. The AI analyzes both, rewrites your resume to highlight relevant skills, match keywords, and optimize formatting for ATS systems. You get a tailored resume in under 60 seconds.
Jobs are automatically sourced from LinkedIn, Indeed, USAJobs.gov, Adzuna, The Muse, Remotive, and more. New listings are refreshed every hour across 97 top companies.
Your account is stored in a secure cloud database (PostgreSQL). Your browser's localStorage is only used to cache your session for speed — clearing it just logs you out, your account and saved data remain intact. We use Google Analytics with IP anonymization and never sell your data.
Your password is hashed with bcrypt (industry-standard, slow-by-design algorithm) before being stored — the actual password is never saved anywhere. Even if the database were breached, passwords cannot be reversed or recovered.
