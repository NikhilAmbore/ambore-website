FAQ

Common questions

Ambore is a free, all-in-one AI career platform with 7 tools: a Job Portal (7,000+ hourly-updated jobs from 97 companies), AI Resume Builder, AI Cover Letter Generator, Job Email Generator, LinkedIn Profile Optimizer, Portfolio Builder, and an AI Interview Co-Pilot for real-time answers during interviews.
Jobs are automatically scraped from 10 sources: LinkedIn (via public API), Indeed (via Apify), USAJobs.gov, Adzuna, The Muse, Remotive, and more. New listings are refreshed every hour across 97 top companies, so you always see the freshest jobs before they fill up.
Upload your existing resume and paste the job description. The AI analyzes both, then rewrites your resume to highlight relevant skills, match keywords, and optimize formatting for ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems). You get a tailored resume in seconds.
Ambore runs entirely in your browser. There are no extensions, no screen sharing, and no external indicators. It works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and in-person interviews — as long as your mic picks up the question.
Everything is free — no credit card, no trial limits. All personal data (resume, sessions, account) is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to external servers beyond the AI API calls needed to generate answers and resumes.
